Navbar card: scrolling, auto-hide and panel menus

A live demo of three options proposed for navbar-card, the Home Assistant navigation bar. The phone runs the real card build from the proposal. Tap around, scroll the page, and flip the options to compare.

Open the demo full screen (best on a phone)

Scrollable bar mobile.scrollable

More routes than fit: swipe the bar sideways. Opening a page keeps its route centred, and the edges fade while there is more to scroll.

Auto-hide mobile.autohide

Slide moves the bar off screen while you scroll down (Material). Minimize shrinks it to a pill showing the current page (iOS 26); tap the pill to expand. Toggle adds an arrow tab to fold the bar away by hand.

Popup style layout.popup_style

Panel shows a popup menu as one list of icon and label rows, with a light dim that leaves the bar usable. Try More in the bar.

Mobile mode mobile.mode

Existing option, here to check the new ones work with both.

The options currently shown, as card YAML.
